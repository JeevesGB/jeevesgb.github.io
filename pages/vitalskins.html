Vital Skins

How to Install

  1. Download the .vitaltheme file for your desired theme.
  2. Open Vital synthesizer and navigate to the Advanced tab.
  3. Scroll down to the Skin section at the bottom.
  4. Click Load Custom Theme... and select the downloaded file.
