Game Background

Option Tuning Car Battle is a series of street-racing games released exclusively in Japan for the PlayStation 1, developed and published by MTO.

The games were created in official collaboration with Option, the legendary Japanese tuning magazine. Featuring real tuning shops such as Veilside, Mines, Re-Amemiya, HKS, Top Secret, and many others.
