Option Tuning Car Battle Research

This page documents research into the game from the perspective of someone new to game modification.

Game Background

Option Tuning Car Battle is a series of street-racing games released exclusively in Japan for the PlayStation 1, developed and published by MTO. The games were created in official collaboration with Option, the legendary Japanese tuning magazine and video publisher that dominated the JDM scene in the 1990s and early 2000s.

Featuring real tuning shops and brands such as Veilside, Mines, Re-Amemiya, HKS, Top Secret, among many others, the series is known for its authentic representation of Japanese street tuning culture. The games include both visual and mechanical damage systems, and various racing types including touge, wangan, and circuit racing.
